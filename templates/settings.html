Understanding Metrics:

  • Precision: Percentage of flagged alerts that are truly anomalous (minimizes false alarms).
  • Recall: Percentage of true anomalies successfully detected (minimizes undetected losses).
  • F1-Score: Harmonic mean balancing precision and recall to assess aggregate classifier viability.
